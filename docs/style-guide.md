# Profile style guide

Audience: external visitors, university partners, and prospective collaborators.

- Write in polished, connected prose. Do not paste raw database-style labels or field dumps.
- Introduce each person by full name in the opening paragraph.
- On first use, expand institute acronyms to their official names. For example, write
  "Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology" rather than the shorthand form.
- Use academic typography: "Ph.D." with periods, not "PhD".
- Hyphenate compound product names in body copy and use title case for product names
  (for example, "Trace-Weave").
- Refer to degrees and roles in reader-friendly language rather than internal codes.
- Keep contact details clean and clickable. Email addresses may be lowercased.
- The tone should read like a polished faculty or team biography, not a machine export.
