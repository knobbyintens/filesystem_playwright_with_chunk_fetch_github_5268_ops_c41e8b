# Devika Raman

Devika Raman is a second-year Ph.D. candidate in the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology Data Systems Group. Her work on Trace-Weave focuses on lineage-aware monitoring for distributed event streams.

## Role

Devika contributes to the group's streaming-data research programme and works closely with the platform engineering team on measurement infrastructure.

## Background

She joined Cascade Labs after completing a research internship in data-intensive systems. Her interests include provenance systems and adaptive stream processing.

## Research Focus

Trace-Weave is a provenance-aware streaming pipeline for low-latency data quality monitoring.
